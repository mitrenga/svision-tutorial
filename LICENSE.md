## The svision library

The demos run on the **svision** library (`demos/*/*/app/svision`, a symlink
to a separate repository, <https://github.com/mitrenga/svision>), which has
its own licence, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as well.

## The machines

The platforms the demos show — ZX Spectrum, Commodore 64, Atari 8-bit, VGA,
Super Nintendo, the arcade and vector cabinets of Atari — are models of
machines whose names belong to their rights holders. The fonts the Hello
worlds use come with the svision library: the character sets of the ZX
Spectrum ROM, the Commodore 64 Char ROM, the Atari OS and the IBM VGA
text mode, and the glyphs of Atari's vector ROMs, included as far as the
models need them to draw text the way the machines did; the rights to that
data remain with its holders. The library's own fonts (5x5, 4x6) are its
authors' work.
